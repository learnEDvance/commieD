# co-up — Shared Pool Mode
*Future Game Mode — Under Consideration*

## 1. Concept

Shared Pool Mode is an alternative version of co-up in which a fixed number of players share a common pool of resources.

Unlike the original mode, players do not join teams through connections. Groups are established as part of the game, and every player in a group draws from the same resource pool.

The central mechanic is collective resource management: every player's actions affect the resources available to everyone else.

## 2. Shared Resources

Each group has a common pool containing resources such as:

- Health (HP)
- Ammunition
- Time (currency)
- Special items and other resources

Resources are consumed and replenished at the group level rather than being managed through individual treasuries and player-to-player transfers.

## 3. Gameplay

Players explore the maze, collect resources, and fight other groups.

- All players in a group share the same resource pool.
- Using resources by one player affects their availability to the entire group.
- Players retain individual movement and combat control.
- Resource management becomes a collective concern.
- There are no player-to-player connections or resource-transfer delays.

## 4. Endgame

The mode retains co-up's last-player-standing objective.

When only one group remains, it is divided into smaller groups. This process repeats until only one player remains.

The rules for distributing resources during division remain to be defined.

## 5. Design Principle

Shared Pool Mode explores a different form of interdependence. Instead of relying on a network of connections to access resources, players share a common supply from the beginning.

Individual decisions have collective consequences, making resource conservation, combat, and survival group-level concerns.

## 6. Status

This mode is a potential future addition to co-up, not a replacement for the original design.

The exact rules for resource regeneration, shared HP, individual deaths, resource allocation during division, and other mechanics remain undecided.
