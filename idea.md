# commieD-Idea

- Multiplayer maze shooter (browser-based)
- Large scale: 1000s of concurrent players across 200 cities globally
- Goal: be the last team standing
-the entire game is minimal in design and assets with the theme of communism(cause commieD=comm(unist)i(com)eD(y))
- Teams are divided uniformly.
- Teams share everything(from ammo, dashes, and even HP)
- If the team HP goes to 0, the entire team dies.
- on a team dying, only the n/2 best players will be revived with team hp also getting halved(number of plrs\*hp per players= team HP, simpool)
- dead players(the n/2 not selected to play), act as ghosts, who search and collect resources for the team.
- on the number of players in team reaching one, the team dies. and is eliminated.

04 — Ghost Mode
- Players who aren't selected for revival become ghosts.
- Ghosts continue contributing to their team's survival.
- Ghosts can collect resources for their team.
- Ghosts can play minigames to generate resources.
- Different resource types have different minigames.
- Ghosts can choose which resource they want to contribute.
05 — Soul Contribution
- Soul is a resource generated by ghosts.
- Souls are generated through a clicker minigame.
- Ghosts must click to contribute their souls to their comrades.
- Souls are converted into HP.
- Generated HP is added to the team's health pool.
- The required clicking rate is 5 CPS.
- At 5 CPS, a ghost generates 1 HP/s(stored, until needed by the live players)
- If the clicking rate falls below 5 CPS, HP is goes down by -0.2HP/s.
- Ghost-generated HP is used when living players have low health.
Health regeneration rules
- Teams without ghosts regenerate HP normally.
- Normal regeneration happens at a fixed rate.
- Normal regeneration does not happen instantly.
- Teams with ghosts cannot regenerate HP normally.
- Teams with ghosts depend on ghost-generated HP instead.
