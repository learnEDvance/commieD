04 — Ghost Mode
- Players who aren't selected for revival become ghosts.
- Ghosts continue contributing to their team's survival.
- Ghosts can collect resources for their team.
- Ghosts can play minigames to generate resources.
- Different resource types have different minigames.
- Ghosts can choose which resource they want to contribute.
05 — Soul Contribution
- Soul is a resource generated by ghosts through a clicker minigame.
- Ghosts click the central soul sprite to contribute their souls to their comrades.
- Souls are converted into HP and added to the team's health pool.
- The clicking rate (CPS) is measured over a rolling 10-second window.
- At 5 CPS or more, a ghost generates 0.2 × CPS HP/s; exactly 5 CPS is 1 HP/s.
- Below 5 CPS, team HP drops by 0.2 HP/s.
- Generated HP is banked until living players need it, with no maximum cap.
- The HP bar is red below 100 × living teammates, yellow up to that baseline + 90, and green above.
- Random shapes appear every 10–30 seconds; clicking one grants +5 HP.
- Ghost-generated HP is used when living players have low health.
- Q pauses and resumes the minigame.
Health regeneration rules
- Teams without ghosts regenerate HP normally.
- Normal regeneration happens at a fixed rate.
- Normal regeneration does not happen instantly.
- Teams with ghosts cannot regenerate HP normally.
- Teams with ghosts depend on ghost-generated HP instead.
06 — Bullet Press
- Follow keyboard instructions to create magazines.
- One sequence fills one magazine; a sequence is 10 characters drawn from A–Z, typed in order.
- A correct key fills the next slot green; a wrong key fills it red and marks the whole magazine broken.
- A broken magazine cannot be discarded; it stays until all 10 slots are filled, then produces nothing.
- The entire sequence must be completed for the next magazine run.
- A perfect magazine produces magazines equal to the multiplier.
- The multiplier is a whole number from 1x to 5x based on average time per key: ≤400 ms is x5, ≤600 ms x4, ≤800 ms x3, ≤1100 ms x2, slower x1.
- A broken magazine awards x0 and increments the broken count.
- Finished magazines eject to the right and the next empty magazine enters from the left.
- takeMagazines() returns whole magazines to the team.
07 --speed dash supplement factory:
- A station-management factory game that produces dash seconds.
- Take empty pots from the unlimited intake and drag them through twelve numbered processes over six reusable stations.
- Each station holds four pots in a 2×2 grid and processes automatically; a pot must be moved before its ready window expires or it spoils and frees its slot.
- A pot can only enter the station for its current process; wrong-station drops are rejected for free.
- Process 12 must finish before the bottler accepts the pot.
- The bottler holds four pots and empties them one at a time over ten seconds each; waiting pots queue.
- One pot produces 20 vials; one vial is one second of dash.
- Vials ride the upper outfeed belt as the pot drains; the emptied pot rides the lower belt away.
- takeVials() / takeSeconds() return the produced dash to the team.
