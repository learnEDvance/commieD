# commieD - Plan

## Core Idea

- Multiplayer maze shooter (browser-based)

- Large scale: 1000s of concurrent players across 200 cities globally

- Goal: be the last team standing

- Teams are divided uniformly.

- Teams share everything(from ammo, dashes, and even HP)

- If the team HP goes to 0, the entire team dies.

- on a team dying, only the n/2 best players will be revived with team hp also getting halved(number of plrs\*hp per players= team HP, simpool)

- dead players(the n/2 not selected to play), act as ghosts, who search and collect resources for the team(other mechanics may be added soon).

- on the number of players in team reaching one, the team dies. and is eliminated.

- What I am thinking for ghost mode is a bunch of minigames for each resource type which the ghosts will have to crank as much as possible to prevent the living players from dying. for example, a ghost needs to click as much as possible to give their "soul" to the player. this soul is then used to create hp for the player. different game for ammo, different game for specials. and the funniest? you can become a ghost shooter. so you cannot move like yourself. but you can shoot at players. so multiple ghosts on 1 player can literally become a 1 man army!

-soul(a pun for literally taking your soul away with clicking). the souls earned is used to create hp. but you need 5cps to get 0.2HP/s. if less than that, you lose hp(muhahahahah!!!!). this hp is used when the player is low on health. if a team has ghosts, it cannot regen normally(muhahaa!). Normal no ghost teams can regen normally. but at a fixed rate, unlike the insta regen in the ghost wale teams.      

-for ghosts choosing to do live shooting. it can choose any teammate, but there can be max no. of ghosts in team/no. of living players in team number of ghosts per player. they act as having seperate guns on the same player body(somewhat like a tank with many guns pointed from it). the ghosts get low damage, stun guns which damage the enemy and reduce their speed by a little(but it is additive per bullet).

-for bullets we will have bullet press, where you work on a factory to literally print bullets and load them into magazines for use.
